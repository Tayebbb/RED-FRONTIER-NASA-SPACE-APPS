# Red Frontier data and artifact sources

## Use policy

Planet-specific mission archives should drive Mars and Moon simulation values. Earth-observation datasets are appropriate for analog terrain, environmental modeling, and machine-learning experiments. Do not present Earth analog data as direct Mars measurements.

|---|---|

## Moon sources

- [LRO](https://lunar.gsfc.nasa.gov/data.html) and [LOLA](https://ode.rsl.wustl.edu/moon/) for terrain and surface data.
- [Diviner](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/diviner.htm) for thermal data.
- [Moon Mineralogy Mapper](https://pds-imaging.jpl.nasa.gov/portal/m3.html) and [Lunar Prospector](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lunarprospector/) for composition.

## Earth analog and ML sources

- [NASA Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/), [Earthdata Search](https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/), [Giovanni](https://giovanni.gsfc.nasa.gov/giovanni/), and [Worldview](https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/).
- [SRTM](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/instruments/srtm), [Landsat](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions), and [ASTER GDEM](https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/products/astgtmv003/) for terrain and land-surface analogs.
- [MODIS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/instruments/modis) products for temperature, aerosols, and cloud context.
- [Copernicus Data Space](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/) and Sentinel-1/2/3/5P for radar, multispectral, thermal, and atmospheric analog data.
