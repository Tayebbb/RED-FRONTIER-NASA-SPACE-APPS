# Final Science and Mast QA

## Scope and verdict

Read-only review of the science/instrument sheet at [science_after.png](science_after.png), the written handoff and prior QA reports, and the nearby geometry/material definitions in [refine_rover.py](refine_rover.py). No Blender scene, source model, exports, or screenshots were changed or created. No existing reference image was viewed again.

**Overall:** The sheet reads coherently as a modular, intentionally low-poly rover. Two functional alignments merit minimal repair: point the radiation detector's sensing face skyward, and close the stated 35 mm ground gap at the soil-contact probe when deployed. The other observed differences are either deliberate abstraction or not established as defects by the available references. Do not describe the generic spectrometer as an exact APXS replica.

## Prioritized required repairs

### P1 — Radiation detector sensing direction

- **Evidence:** In the sheet, `Inst_Radiation` is a compact gray rectangular housing on a dark mounting plate. Its two prominent dark circular apertures are on the vertical presentation face; the top is visually blank. NASA describes Curiosity RAD as deck-mounted and pointed toward the sky. The detector photo below shows an engineering unit, not installed orientation, so the orientation requirement comes from NASA's written instrument description.
- **Minimal repair:** Keep the housing and footprint. Rotate the aperture panel to face up, or add a small upward-facing detector window on the top face and de-emphasize the side apertures. Preserve the low-poly form; no need to reproduce the photographed cylindrical assembly exactly.
- **Bounds/material evidence:** The rendered housing is a small, closed rectangular volume above a plate; dark apertures contrast with gray housing and blue accent. Existing script additions for this part are only small metal details near the base (about 12 mm radius); no numeric outer bound is established in the reviewed evidence.

### P1 — Soil probe ground contact

- **Evidence:** `Inst_Soil` has a two-link articulated arm terminating in a narrow metal probe. The supplied numeric tip height is **Z = 0.035 m** against the ground datum, leaving a 35 mm gap if shown in its deployed/contact pose. That is a functional clearance mismatch for a ground-contact probe, not a low-poly style issue.
- **Minimal repair:** Extend or reposition only the terminal probe by 0.035 m so its working tip reaches Z = 0 in the intended deployed pose. Retain the link, joint and housing shapes; do not rescale the whole instrument.
- **Bounds/material evidence:** The authored plate is 0.4 x 0.4 x 0.05 m; the white instrument housing is 0.28 x 0.28 x 0.18 m. The arm uses narrow metal beams (0.07 m and 0.06 m wide), white joint collars and a metal terminal probe. Keep this footprint and material distinction.

## Limits and image count

- Exact inspected-image count: **3** — `science_after.png`, PIA24175, and PIA13580. No screenshots were taken and no other images were opened.
- Reviewed numerical evidence was limited to the supplied probe/radar coordinates, handoff rover/mast bounds, and explicit geometry dimensions in `refine_rover.py`. Per-object assembled bounds, detector-axis transforms, mesh topology, and material-node values were not independently audited; only visible color/material cues and scripted additions are reported.
- The model is a fictional low-poly rover. The reference photos support broad form/function cues, not exact dimensions or complete flight-hardware fidelity.
